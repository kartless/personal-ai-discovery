# personal-ai-discovery
A prototype and experiments for a persistent, personalized AI cultural discovery
## What is this?

This is an experimental prototype exploring whether a persistent AI model of an individual's preferences can provide substantially more personalized search and discovery than conventional recommendation systems.

The original experiment focused on books. Rather than simply asking an LLM to recommend books based on genre or previously liked titles, I spent an extended conversation explaining what I actually like and dislike about literature—including preferences about plot structure, characters, humor, and other qualitative characteristics.

The resulting recommendations were surprisingly good. That led me to build a small prototype for maintaining an evolving preference model over time and using it to guide future recommendations.

The broader idea is that the same approach could potentially be applied to cultural discovery more generally, and perhaps eventually to search itself.
## Project status

This is a proof-of-concept and an exploration, not a production-ready application.

I am a writer rather than a software engineer, and I am publishing this because I think the underlying idea is interesting enough to be worth exploring by people with more technical expertise than I have. I am not currently trying to turn this into a business or become the project's primary developer.

If you find the concept interesting, feel free to experiment with it, critique it, or build on it.
